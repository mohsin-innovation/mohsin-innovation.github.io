Privacy Policy

Fitness Challenge

1. Introduction

Welcome to Fitness Challenge (“we”, “our”, or “us”). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how our app handles information and permissions.

2. Information We Collect

Our app does not require you to create an account or provide any personally identifiable information. We do not store or share any personal data with third parties.

The app may collect minimal and anonymous data locally on your device, such as:

  • Step count data for activity tracking.
  • Basic app usage statistics (e.g., number of steps, hydration reminders, and challenge progress).

3. Permissions Used

To provide accurate step counting and wellness tracking, our app requests the following permission: 

<uses-permission android:name="android.permission.ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION"/>

Purpose: This permission is used solely to detect and count your physical activity steps for fitness tracking. We do not collect, transmit, or share this data outside your device.

4. How We Use Information

The information collected by the app is used exclusively for:

  • Displaying your step count and activity statistics.
  • Providing motivational insights and daily challenges.
  • Sending optional reminders for hydration and movement.

All data remains on your device and is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app.

5. Account Deletion

Our app does not create or manage user accounts. Therefore, no account deletion is required. If you wish to remove all stored data, simply uninstall the app from your device — this will erase all associated data.

6. Third-Party Services

The app does not use third-party analytics, ads, or tracking tools. Your activity data is private and remains within your device.

7. Data Security

We take your privacy seriously. Since no personal or sensitive data leaves your device, your information remains secure and under your control at all times.

8. Children’s Privacy

Our app is suitable for general audiences and does not target children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be reflected in this document and will take effect immediately upon posting.
